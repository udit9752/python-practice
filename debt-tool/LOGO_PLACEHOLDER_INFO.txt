IMPORTANT: YOUR BRAND LOGO
================================================================================

The system detected a brand logo image in your initial request. To properly 
integrate your logo into the Debt Freedom Analyzer tool, please follow these 
instructions:

1. SAVE YOUR LOGO IMAGE:
   - The logo image you provided should be saved as "logo.png"
   - Recommended size: 200x200 pixels
   - Format: PNG with transparent background (preferred)
   - File size: Under 50KB after optimization

2. WHERE TO PLACE YOUR LOGO:

   For Standalone Website:
   ----------------------
   /public_html/logo.png

   For WordPress Plugin:
   --------------------
   /wp-content/plugins/debt-freedom-analyzer/assets/images/logo.png
   
   Also upload to:
   /wp-content/uploads/logo.png

3. THE LOGO WILL APPEAR IN:
   - Header (top left with company name)
   - Footer (bottom of page)
   - Print version of roadmaps

4. LOGO COLORS DETECTED:
   Based on your logo, we've used these colors throughout the design:
   - Primary Teal: #0F5F5C (dark teal/green)
   - Gold Accent: #F4C430 (yellow/gold)
   - Dark Teal: #0A3F3D (darker shade)

5. IF YOU NEED TO ADJUST COLORS:
   Edit the file: /debt-tool/styles.css
   Find the :root section and update:
   
   :root {
       --primary-teal: #0F5F5C;    /* Change to your color */
       --primary-gold: #F4C430;    /* Change to your color */
       --primary-dark: #0A3F3D;    /* Change to your color */
   }

6. OPTIMIZE YOUR LOGO:
   Use these free tools to optimize your logo for web:
   - TinyPNG.com - Compress without quality loss
   - Squoosh.app - Advanced compression
   - Canva.com - Resize and edit

7. NEED HELP?
   See the complete guide: /debt-tool/LOGO_SETUP.md
